Anthony Karam

Anthony Karam is an engineer and children’s book creator in Chandler, Arizona, originally from Lexington, Kentucky.

He creates alphabet coloring books that help children learn letters while celebrating local places, landmarks, animals, and community life.

About the books

Each alphabet coloring book pairs simple A-to-Z entries with bold, kid-friendly line art designed for coloring.

The books are created for families, homeschoolers, classrooms, and anyone who enjoys place-based children’s activities.
